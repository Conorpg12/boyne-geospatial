About Me

Conor Gleeson

I'm a GIS professional. My experience covers field data collection, spatial analysis, data management and map production.

Before moving home to Ireland, I spent two years as a GIS Analyst in rural New South Wales, Australia, where I ran fieldwork in remote areas and set up spatial data collection methods.

I also fly a drone for small mapping projects.

I live in Navan and run Boyne Geospatial in my spare time. I use it to post mini projects, share free tools, and take on a small amount of map design, data and drone work.

Why Boyne?
