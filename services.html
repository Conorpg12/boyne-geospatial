Work With Me

Map Design, GIS Data & Drone Imagery

Alongside my own projects, I take on a small amount of independent work.

What I Can Help With

Suited to small businesses, sports clubs, community and heritage groups, researchers and individuals.

Map Design & Cartography

Maps designed for print or screen, at whatever scale the job needs. Get in touch.

  • Thematic and reference maps
  • Wall maps and framed prints
  • Walking, running and event route maps
  • Maps and graphics for publications and reports

Spatial Analysis & Data

Cleaning up, converting and analysing spatial data, and automating tasks you do often. Get in touch.

  • Cleaning, converting and validating spatial data
  • Turning spreadsheets and GPS tracks into usable maps
  • Exploring and visualising open datasets
  • Python and QGIS automation of repetitive workflows
  • Simple interactive web maps

Drone Imagery

Aerial photography and video, flown in line with Irish Aviation Authority rules. Get in touch.

  • Landscape and heritage aerial photography
  • Club, community and event photo and video content
  • Framed aerial prints of any location, on request
  • Small orthomosaics for personal, research and community projects

What I Don't Take On

Boyne Geospatial is run separately from my full-time job. I don't accept work involving:

  • Planning applications, appeals, objections or submissions of any kind
  • Site suitability, zoning or development feasibility assessments
  • Work for, on behalf of, or in dispute with any local authority
  • Any project that requires a decision from a local authority
